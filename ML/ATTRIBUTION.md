# Third-party models and data

- **roberta-base-go_emotions-onnx** by Sam Lowe: https://huggingface.co/SamLowe/roberta-base-go_emotions-onnx (MIT License).
  ONNX export of https://huggingface.co/SamLowe/roberta-base-go_emotions, fine-tuned from roberta-base on GoEmotions.
- **GoEmotions** dataset and sentiment mapping by Google Research: https://github.com/google-research/google-research/tree/master/goemotions (Apache License 2.0).

Model files are downloaded into `ML/models/` (git-ignored) and are not stored in this repository.
